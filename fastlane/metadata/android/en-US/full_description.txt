<p>InnerDesk puts Samsung DeX or Pixel Android 16 desktop on this screen. Not HDMI. Not a TV.</p>
<p>Do one-time setup (notifications, display over other apps, unrestricted battery, Accessibility), then pair <b>once per boot</b> with Wireless debugging (Shizuku works too). Tap Start desktop, and the desktop fills the phone. Unfolded Folds go fullscreen; tabletop splits desktop on top and a trackpad below. Regular phones work in portrait or landscape.</p>
<p><b>What you need</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Pixel on Android 16, or Samsung on One UI 8 (Android 16) with DeX</li>
<li>The InnerDesk accessibility service</li>
<li>Display over other apps, notifications, and unrestricted battery</li>
<li>Developer options → Wireless debugging, once this boot (no root)</li>
</ul>
<p>After the privileged daemon is up you can turn Wireless debugging off. Pair again after a reboot. Other devices are not supported yet.</p>
<p>Optional anonymous usage and crash reports (random id only) are off by default in this F-Droid build. Turn them on under About → Privacy if you want. Pairing PINs are never sent.</p>
